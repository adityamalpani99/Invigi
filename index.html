Study accountability · on-device ML

Set your goal. The invigilator learns where your books live — and calls you back when you truly drift.

Invigi runs adaptive on-device machine learning: it learns your personal study areas — screen, notebook, textbook — tells genuine drift (phone, absence, noise, zoned-out staring) apart from reading and writing, and sounds a siren that keeps sounding until you're back at work.

Privacy, plainly stated

  • All sensing and all ML happen in your browser. Camera frames and audio are analyzed in memory and discarded — nothing is uploaded, ever.
  • Nothing is recorded. Audio is reduced to anonymous signal levels — never content. The adaptive model stores only anonymous angle numbers describing where your study areas are.
  • Only session summaries and those angle numbers are saved — in this browser, on your own computer. Export or erase them anytime.
  • How it decides: it learns your study zones continuously, checks for reading-like eye movement before judging an unfamiliar direction, detects phones with a real object detector plus a screen-flicker analyzer, and flags sustained loud audio (media, conversation). Leaving the desk (~20 s) or a phone in view (~7 s) sounds the siren until you return.

AI models (~7 MB) are fetched once from a public CDN and cached. Internet is needed the first time; after that, everything is offline and local.
